The company added that the model does not cross its &quot;Critical&quot; cybersecurity risk threshold, which could bring &quot;unprecedented new pathways to severe harm,&quot; but it does meet the criteria for its &quot;High&quot; risk classification, which could &quot;amplify existing pathways to severe harm.&quot; &quot;GPT-5.5 underwent extensive third-party safeguard testing and red teaming for cyber and bio [risks], and we&#x27;ve been iterating on our cyber safeguards for months with increasingly cyber capable models,&quot; Mia Glaese, OpenAI&#x27;s vice president of research, said during the briefing on Thursday. The cybersecurity risks presented by AI have been top of mind for tech executives and government officials since Anthropic announced its Mythos model earlier this month. The company decided to limit Mythos&#x27; rollout because of its ability to identifying weaknesses and security flaws within software. GPT-5.5 is rolling out to OpenAI&#x27;s paid subscribers, including its Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users, in ChatGPT and its coding assistant Codex on Thursday.
