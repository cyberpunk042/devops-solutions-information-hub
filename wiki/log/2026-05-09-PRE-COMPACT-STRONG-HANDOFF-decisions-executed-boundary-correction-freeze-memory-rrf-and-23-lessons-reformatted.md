## Infrastructure Landed This Arc

### 5-channel RRF retrieval (Decision E)

**File**: `tools/wiki_search.py` (~340 LOC pure Python, no new deps)

**Channels** (each ranked independently before RRF merge):

| # | Channel | Mechanism | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Exact fact-key | title / aliases / tags exact-or-substring match | **2.0** (strongest) |
| 2 | FTS | Porter-stemmed tokenization, query-token coverage + density | 1.0 |
| 3 | Vector | TF-IDF cosine similarity (stemmed corpus) | 1.0 |
| 4 | HyDE | TF-IDF cosine on templated hypothetical-doc expansion | 1.0 |
| 5 | Raw body | Case-insensitive substring (verbatim safety net) | 0.3 (lowest) |

**Reciprocal Rank Fusion**: `score = sum(weight_i / (rrf_k + rank_i + 1))` with `rrf_k=60`. Cloudflare-pattern weights.

**MCP wiring**: `wiki_search(query, k=10, diagnostics=False)` delegates to `tools.wiki_search.search`.

**Future upgrade path** (operator-decision when needed): swap TF-IDF for neural embeddings by replacing `_TfIdfIndex` internals. Options: sentence-transformers (~1.5GB w/ PyTorch) · fastembed (~200MB ONNX) · Ollama embeddings (uses operator's existing Ollama) · OpenAI/Anthropic API.
