## Why Phase 10 emerged organically

Phase 10 was NOT planned at body-of-work outset. It emerged organically as authoring continued post-Fire 52. The driver: each cross-cutting piece resolved a specific gap that became visible AFTER Phase 1-9 substrate was complete:

- Phase 1-9 produced 52 pieces of per-axis substantive work
- Operator-empirical questions surfaced: "how do these pieces relate?" "what's the priority?" "what's the workflow?"
- Phase 10 pieces answer those questions — building INTEGRATION substrate atop SUBSTANTIVE substrate

This is the canonical pattern of mature wiki-LLM body-of-work: substrate first, integration second. Per piece #18 stress-testing-as-validation: this layered approach lets each tier prove independently before promotion.
