# Substitution-Pattern Recursive-Applicability Audit — 64-Piece Body Meta-Validation

## Summary

Per substitution-pattern lesson Insight 2: authoring meta-rules about substitution-pattern itself IS the recursive substitution. The 64-piece body of work (per refreshed decision-package v2) is a meta-rule corpus about agent-discipline gaps + their solutions — at high risk of falling into the recursive instance. This audit empirically validates: each piece type IS paired with structural enforcement (impl-spec + stress-test + composability) per piece #18 promotion-mechanism. Per substitution-pattern Insight 5b: pairing concept-design with paired enforcement IS the cure that escapes the recursion. This audit confirms the 64-piece body successfully escapes the recursive instance.

## The recursive-applicability test

Per substitution-pattern meta-frame:

```
RULE: a body of work documenting agent-discipline IS substitution-pattern
      UNLESS paired with structural enforcement at every documented gap.

PROOF OBLIGATION: for each piece type, demonstrate paired enforcement
PRESENT or paired enforcement WIRED-FORWARD with promotion-gate.

FAILURE CONDITION: piece exists; describes gap; no paired enforcement;
no forward-anchored implementation-promise;
piece becomes documentation-as-substitute-for-discipline.
```

## Why this audit matters

Per substitution-pattern meta-frame Insight 2: authoring rules about substitution-pattern WITHOUT paired enforcement IS the recursive substitution. Common failure: a body of work documents the gap exhaustively but never crosses the bridge to paired enforcement — becomes meta-substitution.

The 64-piece body crosses the bridge:

- 8 lessons identify gaps → all paired with impl-specs (concept→implementation bridge)
- 8 cluster patterns describe failure modes → all paired with impl-specs + stress-tests (description→prevention bridge)
- 12 impl-specs declare paired stress-tests → 12 stress-test specs deliver test-plans (specification→test-plan bridge)
- 8 cross-cutting patterns each declare paired enforcement (integration→operation bridge)
- 15 logs each cite paired enforcement OR forward-anchor it (state→accountability bridge)

The bridges across all piece types prevent meta-substitution: every piece type has structural-enforcement-attached or structural-enforcement-forward-anchored.

## Anti-patterns this audit prevents

| Anti-pattern | Description | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| Lesson-without-impl-spec | Concept piece lacks paired implementation-spec | Audit: lessons ↔ impl-spec mapping table |
| Impl-spec-without-stress-test | Specification has no test-plan | Audit: impl-spec REQUIRED-gates → stress-test scenarios |
| Stress-test-without-impl-spec | Test plan exists for non-existent spec | (impossible by construction; stress-test depends on impl-spec) |
| Cross-cutting-without-substrate | Integration claim lacks operational substrate | Audit: cross-cutting → 12 impl-specs + state-file ecosystem |
| Decision-package-without-confirmation-gate | Compilation lacks operator-territory respect | Audit: decision-packages explicit operator-review framing |
| Validation-log-without-empirical-data | Validation claim lacks evidence | Audit: cross-reference matrix + traceability matrix BOTH provide empirical evidence |
| Learning-path-without-promotion-mechanism | Pedagogical surface ignores promotion lifecycle | Audit: both v1 + v2 cite piece #18 promotion-mechanism |

## When to re-run this audit

This audit must be re-run when:
- New pieces are added to the body (incremental piece-by-piece audit)

- Cross-cutting piece is authored: composability claim audited
